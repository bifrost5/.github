# Bifrost5

A suite of platforms for agentic engineering.

## Foundry

Keeps your software moving while you sleep. Foundry is a platform for multi-day agentic engineering: you give it goals and tasks, it runs AI agents in separate workspaces, sends failures back to be fixed against fixed tests, and keeps going through crashes and restarts.

[Foundry on GitHub](https://github.com/bifrost5/foundry) · [More about Foundry](https://bifrost5.com/foundry)

## EdgeKit

Put your apps online from your own hardware, private by default. Open source under the MIT license. Foundry runs on EdgeKit since October 2026.

[EdgeKit on GitHub](https://github.com/bifrost5/edge-kit) · [More about EdgeKit](https://bifrost5.com/edgekit)
